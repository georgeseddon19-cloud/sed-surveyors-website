Before your surveyor arrives

  • Make sure the surveyor can access every room, including lofts, cellars, and outbuildings.
  • Unlock or point out access to the meter cupboard, loft hatch, and any boarded lofts.
  • Have proof of any insulation, glazing, or heating upgrades ready (receipts, certificates, FENSA/Gas Safe numbers).
  • Clear space around the boiler, meters, and hot water cylinder.

Documents to have ready

  • Proof of address / property ownership or tenancy
  • Any previous EPC or survey report for the property
  • Planning permission or building control documents for extensions/conversions
  • Details of any renewable technology (solar PV, heat pump, etc.)

Start to finish

  1. BookPick a slot in the calendar above — Monday to Friday, 16:00–20:00.
  2. Site visitYour surveyor visits for 45 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes, depending on property size and service.
  3. Produce & QAYour report or certificate is produced and quality-checked.
  4. PaymentYour certificate or report is lodged and the result released once payment is received.
  5. Lodged to the registerFor EPCs, your certificate is lodged to the national register with a direct link to view or download it.

Note: the "same-day outcome" guarantee on the homepage applies once payment has been received — see step 4 above.
